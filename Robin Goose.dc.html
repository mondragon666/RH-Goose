02 — ORIGIN

It started with
one bad goose

Goose #0001 was drawn as a joke: black head, pink beak, background set to the loudest green in the picker. The joke did not stop. Two thousand geese later, the green stayed.

Every goose is 16×16 pixels, hand-placed, no generator smoothing anything over. The rarest trait in the whole flock is a single pair of blue boots. Nine geese have them. Nobody knows why.

“There is no whitepaper. There is a goose, and the goose is honking.”
